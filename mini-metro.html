How to Play

GoalKeep passengers moving, prevent stations from overcrowding, and grow a balanced network before your line breaks down.

  • Click a station, then another station to add a route.
  • Use the station count and line count to judge where your network is under pressure.
  • Add trains and carriages to match demand, especially at busy hubs.
  • Watch peak-time crowding carefully. A congested station can end a run fast.

Good Strategies

  • Build a strong central backbone before branching into lots of smaller lines.
  • Expand near busy transfer points instead of spreading capacity evenly everywhere.
  • Keep lines simple and direct so passengers do not get stuck in loops or dead ends.
  • Prioritize upgrades where demand is highest and where crowding is starting to build.
  • Use a balanced network with a few strong connections rather than many weak ones.

Difficulty Modes

  • Easy: no river obstacle, easier routing, more forgiving start.
  • Hard: standard mode with the river present, creating more difficult route planning.
